Questions teams ask before they trust the workflow.

What does this image-to-angle workflow actually generate?

It generates new product views from one uploaded image so you can present the item from more than one angle.

Do I need a CAD file or a real 3D model first?

No. The workflow is based on an uploaded image plus angle controls, not a traditional 3D asset pipeline.

Can the output be used in product pages and ads?

Yes. The generated images are intended for commercial use cases such as product detail pages, campaign creative, and listings.

How is this different from a generic AI image editor?

The product is opinionated around angle generation. The UI, prompt logic, and result framing are optimized for directional control.

Overview

What this tool does

imgcraft AI is an AI product angle generator for product teams. Start with one image, control the viewing direction with X/Y/Z, and generate new angles without a full 3D workflow.

What does this image to 3D converter actually generate?

It generates new product views from a single uploaded image so you can present the item from more than one angle.

Do I need a CAD file or a real 3D model first?

No. The workflow is based on an uploaded image plus angle controls, not a traditional 3D asset pipeline.

Can the output be used in product pages and ads?

Yes. The generated images are intended for commercial use cases such as product detail pages, campaign creative, and listings.
